Background背景

Jia Li | 李嘉

I am an undergraduate studying Data Science and Big Data Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research interests are in robot learning and reinforcement learning, with a focus on whole-body control and humanoid loco-manipulation.我是华中科技大学数据科学与大数据技术专业本科生，研究兴趣是机器人学习与强化学习，关注全身控制和人形机器人移动操作。

I am particularly interested in learning reusable behavioral representations and skills from motion and interaction data. My recent work spans humanoid motion tracking, unsupervised skill learning and learning loco-manipulation from human videos. I hope to build robots that acquire diverse skills and use them flexibly in new tasks.我尤其关注如何从运动与交互数据中学习可复用的行为表征与技能。近期研究涉及人形机器人动作跟踪、无监督技能学习，以及从人类视频中学习移动操作。我希望机器人能够掌握多样的技能，并在新的任务中灵活运用。
